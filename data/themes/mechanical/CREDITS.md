# Mechanical theme artwork

## Keycaps: `icons/caps/`

Adapted from [Free Keyboard Graphics and Key Icons for Screencasts](https://github.com/q2apro/keyboard-keys-speedflips)
by q2apro (speedflips.com), version 1.1, commit `26cb04c`. That work is itself
derived from [Computer keyboard US.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_keyboard_US.svg)
on Wikimedia Commons, by Mysid (Computer keyboard Danish layout.svg), with a derivative work by Incnis Mrsi.

**License: public domain.** Free for any use.

Changes: the Arial labels and the drawn arrows were removed. The Enter cap uses the
Backspace shape, and the Space cap uses the right Shift shape. Legends from Misonocons
(below) were added to the Shift, Caps, Tab, Backspace, Enter, Ctrl, Alt and Super caps.

## Key legends: `icons/*.svg` and the legends on the caps

Adapted from [Misonocons](https://github.com/misonoworks/misonocons) (thick variant)
by MisonoWorks, commit `ad189ff`.

**License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)**,
full text in `LICENSE-misonocons.txt`. Misonocons by MisonoWorks is licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Changes: single icons were cut out of the icon sheet and resized. On the caps they're
recolored to `#2b2b2b`.

## Device blanks: `icons/caps/_mouse.svg`, `_touchpad.svg`, `_pen.svg`

Drawn for Kiwi, in the colors and lighting of the keycaps above. Same license as Kiwi.
